The Long Crawl

A gameplay prototype built in Unreal Engine 5 centered around custom turtle movement, traversal mechanics, and state-based enemy AI.

Overview

The Long Crawl is an Unreal Engine 5 gameplay prototype where I explored custom character movement, traversal mechanics, enemy behavior, and reusable gameplay systems.

The project focuses on a turtle character with movement behavior affected by the environment, alongside multiple enemy types and NPCs driven by state-based AI logic.

Technical Features

Custom Movement

Developed custom player movement with slope-based behavior and friction to make traversal react to the terrain.

Dash & Stamina

Implemented dash and stamina mechanics that integrate with the player's movement and traversal systems.

Enemy AI

Built state-based behavior for multiple enemy types, including crab and bird enemies with different gameplay behaviors.

Reusable Systems

Used reusable Unreal components and interfaces to share gameplay behavior between actors while keeping systems modular.

AI & Gameplay

NPC turtles navigate toward their goal while enemy actors use state-based logic to control their behavior. Different enemy types can implement their own behavior while sharing reusable gameplay systems where appropriate.

Building the prototype gave me experience structuring gameplay code in Unreal Engine and separating movement, AI, and actor behavior into systems that could be reused as the prototype expanded.
